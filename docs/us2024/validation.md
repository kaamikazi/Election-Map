## Artifacts and limitations

Six PNGs were generated by `scripts/make-us2024-posts.js` through the browser's normal `compositePair` / `composite` / `drawMap` path. Their hashes, saved-document/source-PDF hashes and each pair's exact projection and 51-unit visibility reports were independently verified against `manifest.json`. Fonts, flag badge, seal and reference geometry load before layout/export. The selected working theme is restored after paired rendering.

Square/portrait keep minor totals below the map; widescreen uses a measured side panel. All categories and citations are retained. The USA composite projection uses repositioned Alaska/Hawaii insets, reduces Alaska's scale and clips distant Aleutian islands; these limits are stated in every PNG. The Natural Earth state/DC outline is generalized 1:10m reference geometry, with geographic vintage not stated. Electoral districts are not depicted.

The complete repository suite is **not claimed green**. The earlier Athens full run recorded 152/160 passing, with five missing ignored raw geometry fixtures, one reproducible Bangladesh browser-promise failure and two intermittent browser/timeout cases. Unchanged-baseline and isolated reruns are documented in [the Athens validation report](../athens/validation.md). Those unrelated failures were not repaired or skipped for this national post.

No PR merge, deployment or social posting was performed automatically.
