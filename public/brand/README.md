# History Through Maps identity

Copied from [kaamikazi/History-C](https://github.com/kaamikazi/History-C/tree/d74096413ba56cc04a586daf1ab5bfe66582720d), identity version 2: tokens.json, mark.svg and mark-on-dark.svg. `src/identity.js` adapts these tokens to the election renderer. The source tokens remain here for comparison when the channel identity changes.

Paper = light; Lamplight = dark. Ground, sea, unassigned land, rules, type and the channel seal follow the channel. Election party colours, metric ramps, flags and the existing “no party family” hatch retain their data meanings. The history thumbnail rule of one red territory therefore does not apply to election results.

Fonts are bundled, with their SIL Open Font License notices, from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/51303ca9e8ac9dcea7b12d307ba568fd0e6fcfca/ofl):

- IMFeENit.ttf: imfellenglish/IMFeENit28P.ttf, italic display.
- EBGaramond.ttf: ebgaramond/EBGaramond[wght].ttf, variable body face.
- CormorantSC.ttf: cormorantsc/CormorantSC-SemiBold.ttf, small-cap labels.

The filenames are local aliases; font family names and font contents are unchanged. Loading is explicit and checked before export, restoration or series planning. No Google Fonts request is needed at runtime.
